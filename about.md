## What do I do here?
It's my personal mind in public, I mostly write technical articles about Linux, programming and (soon) science. I will soon write mathematical demonstrations of my experiments without going to far into a proper paper. I do NOT use LLMs to write this since I trully believe creativity comes from a human mind, not a NLP model designed to replicate a human model. I don't get paid to write this and probably never will. This is a personal project I've been maintaining since December 2022 and has a very special place in my heart to me. I'm a major graduate, currently studying and pursuing a master and later, PhD in Science and 
Quantum Physics / Biology. In the meantime, I read, write my journals and book's notes handwritten (I prefer handwritten notes over .nb sheets but, I'm alright with both). I also like maintaining servers and .deb and .xbps packages. I really tend to write everyday. 

I've decided to split my writing architecture. On my [Substack](https://elyayoveloz.substack.com/) I will write shorter articles about life, that open up that opportunity to debate and think. In my personal site, I will write longer articles dedicated to my experiments, tutorials, demonstrations and some jokes about Quantum Mechanics as well hehe. I decided to split it since mixing things for me and throwing everything I thinking into here will confuse me and for the sake of my mind, separating them is the way to maintain some inner peace. The existing articles that have mental notes and lessons will prevail, don't worry.  

I have a home-lab for simulations and my stuff (my obsidian livesync server, my cloud, navidrome, SearXNG instance, etc..). I'm very grateful for those people who kindly gave me this hardware so I can test and deploy my own stuff. I also maintain and build my inner self-kid 
mentality, a curious mentality, where I can ask myself a lot of things without losing wonder. I love the uncertainty of this universe. I love this universe, I love nature, plants, trees, forests, grass and I'm a trully advocator of nature. Nature is still an important part in our lives. I also love taking care of Nella (see below) and talking to them. I don't like and believe in modern consumerism, I believe our stuff has a special place in us.

Despite the case whatsoever, I hope you all have a great time visiting my site and don't doubt to reach me in my [contact](https://its-yayo.github.io/contact) page
