## What do I do here?
It's my personal workspace in public, I mostly write technical articles about Linux, programming and (soon) science. I do NOT use LLMs to write this since I trully believe creativity comes from a human mind, not a NLP model designed to replicate a human model. I also tend to write some mental notes in here, as you all can see. Some personal, deep,
inspirational notes not to give up, to not be quitter and to trascend limits, with friends, relationships or maybe in solitude. I don't get paid to write this and probably never will. This is a personal project I've been maintaining since December 2022 and has a very special place in my heart to me. I'm a major graduate, currently pursuing a Mater and later, PhD in Science and 
Quantum Physics / Biology. In the meantime, I read, write my journals and book's notes handwritten (I prefer handwritten notes over .nb sheets but, I'm alright with both). I also like maintaining servers and .deb and .xbps packages. I really tend to write everyday. 

I have a home-lab for simulations and my stuff (my obsidian livesync server, my cloud, navidrome, SearXNG instance, etc..). I'm very grateful for those people who kindly gave me this hardware so I can test and deploy my own stuff. I also maintain and build my inner self-kid 
mentality, a curious mentality, where I can ask myself a lot of things without losing wonder. I love the uncertainty of this universe. I love this universe, I love nature, plants, trees, forests, grass and I'm a trully advocator of nature. Nature is still an important part in our lives. I also love taking care of my most personal totems and talking to them. I don't like and believe in modern consumerism, I believe our stuff has a special place in us.

Despite the case whatsoever, I hope you all have a great time visiting my site and don't doubt to reach me in my [contact](https://its-yayo.github.io/contact) page
